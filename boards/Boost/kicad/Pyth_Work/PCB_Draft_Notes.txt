PLACEMENT AND ROUTING
L1, Q1 and D1 form the switching section. D1 is adjacent to the high-voltage
C8 ceramic. The Q1 source current returns through R9; the sense-filter branch
leaves the shunt's source-side pad separately through a Kelvin branch. Gate drive
uses a short two-via back-layer path around the VCC decoupling connection. The
bottom plane is HV_BOTTOM. U1 thermal/return vias are outside its exposed-pad
solder opening. All vias are 0.60 mm / 0.30 mm drill and tented on both sides.

General copper clearance is0.20 mm; /SW and /HV_TOP to other nets use0.50 mm.
Copper-to-edge minimum is0.50 mm. These are explicit bench-layout constraints,
not an insulation or end-product qualification. The high-voltage output trace
uses the board perimeter and is separated from the feedback/compensation area.
The controller's small-pitch lands retain their manufacturer package geometry.

Q1 uses the verified onsemi CASE511DH recommended copper lands. Its two flank
no-copper regions are board-level rule areas at local x[-1.7,-1.185] and
[1.185,1.7], y[-1.3,0.4] relative to Q1 (145,102.5 mm). This preserves the
manufacturer restriction while avoiding a KiCad footprint-zone serialization
translation defect found during checks. The keepouts prohibit tracks, vias and
pours; no unrelated pad overlaps them. Q1 pad1 is lower-left in the normal top
view. The four-window exposed-pad paste is an engineering starting point for
assembly review. Moving Q1 later requires moving these two board rule areas.

C5 is Panasonic EEUEE2C101S: 100uF160V, 7.5 mm lead pitch. Reserve its maximum
16.5 mm body diameter,22 mm body height and the manufacturer's vent clearance.
The thin board needs suitable mechanical support for this large radial part.
No mount-hole changes were introduced without a product enclosure definition.
