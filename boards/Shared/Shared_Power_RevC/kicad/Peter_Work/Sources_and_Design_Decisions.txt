The four isolated modules have no minimum-load requirement, eliminating the
old2.88W total200-ohm preloads. Their83% efficiency is at nominal input/full
load; no guaranteed5V/light-load efficiency is asserted. The scenario budget
uses stated65-75% estimates for them and60% for the monitor module bank.
The two logic banks follow the0.47uH TI application with short switch loops,
local input/output ceramics, a quiet left-side feedback divider and separate
outputs. Both are fixed-PWM enabled when powered. No patient signal, HV stage,
battery charger or raw-cell protection circuitry is placed on this board.
