RAIL SPLITTER - ENGINEERING BENCH PROTOTYPE

Open RailSplitter_Electrical_Draft.kicad_pro in KiCad10. The schematic, PCB and local libraries
are included. The routed PCB matches the schematic and passes the checks in
PCB_DRC.json / PCB_Validation.json. Read PCB_Draft_Notes.txt for mechanics,
pinout and layout decisions, Draft_Review.txt for electrical assumptions, and
Fabrication_Order_Notes.txt for the0.4 mm bare-board order. The top-level upload
archive is RailSplitter_Prototype_JLCPCB.zip. The hardware still requires assembly and bench testing.
