SIGNAL GENERATOR REV C — BARE-PCB BENCH PROTOTYPE
17 October 2026

Upload SignalGen_Prototype_RevC_JLCPCB.zip as one design. It has seven Gerber
layers and one plated-hole Excellon file at the ZIP root. There are no
non-plated holes; the empty NPTH export is omitted from the upload ZIP.
This is a bare-PCB package. Component_Positions.csv is a placement reference,
not an approved JLCPCB assembly order or a substitute for a reviewed BOM.

ORDER SETTINGS
2 layers; FR-4; 0.4 mm nominal thickness; 1 oz outer copper; ENIG;
green solder mask; white silkscreen; tent vias on both sides.
Outline centerline: 74.676 x 95.902 mm. The original native design thickness
is 0.41116 mm; 0.4 mm is the fabricator's nominal option. Do not choose 1.6 mm.
The Gerber job uses a stroke-inclusive bounding box and unspecified finish;
it is retained as an engineering record outside the upload ZIP. Select the
finish and nominal thickness explicitly in the order form.

COMPLETED FILE CHECKS
Rev C corrects all 16 U4/U7 lands to TI's 1.40 x 0.45 mm example with
0.65 mm pitch and 0.05 mm corner radius. Adjacent copper clearance is now
0.20 mm. The old 0.18 mm package exception was removed; no DRC exclusions
or new ignored checks were added. Open the complete extracted .kicad_pro
project with its local libraries; a lone PCB file omits that library context.

77 components, 217 pads, 213 connected pins, 822 trace segments and 47 vias.
Zero native DRC violations, zero unconnected items and zero schematic parity
issues. Three separate ground planes and a channel keepout are present.
All connector pad dimensions/drills and the outline are retained. A 1 nm
import-rounding difference at J1 is below the 0.00001 mm comparison precision.
Measured projected copper gaps are at least 3.628 mm primary-to-secondary
and 5.600 mm between the two secondary domains. These are geometric checks,
not insulation-system or medical isolation qualification.
All 77 references are on front silkscreen; minimum text is 1.0 mm with
0.16 mm strokes. DAC pin 1 is explicitly marked. Obstructing body-outline
segments were removed while retaining assembly outlines on the fab layer.
Independent Gerber geometry and every emitted drill position/diameter were
checked against the native board. See FABRICATION_VERIFICATION.json.

ASSEMBLY AND BENCH TEST
Keep both secondary grounds separate from the primary ground and each other.
J1/J3 form channel 1; J2/J4 form channel 2. See the integration connection map.
The old DAC8411 firmware must be changed for DAC8830 framing and clock timing.
Check low-voltage outputs independently before integrating the HV chain.
Reference ripple, DC offset, IA0305S light-load rails, waveform fidelity and
startup behavior still require real measurements. The design does not include
the complete system's output limits, inhibit or electrode-disconnect sensing.
This is a dummy-load development board; it is not for connection to a person.
No JLCPCB online checker run or physical test has been claimed.
