================================================================================
CRITICAL RESTRICTIONS
================================================================================
* FLASH/PSRAM (X): GPIO 26-32 are internal. DO NOT USE.
* STRAPPING PINS:  GPIO 0, 3, 45, 46 affect boot mode.
* NATIVE USB:      GPIO 19, 20 are reserved for USB interface.
* UART0:           GPIO 43, 44 are used for Serial/USB bridge.
