RAIL SPLITTER

R1/R2: Vishay TNPW0805100KBEEA. Each is 100 kohm, 0.1%, 25 ppm/K, 0805. The current lead-free TNPW e3 datasheet gives 150 V maximum working voltage and general-operation P70 of 0.140 W for 0805; this supplement uses the conservative existing 0.125 W design limit. At nominal 80 V bus each resistor sees about 40 V and dissipates 16 mW. The native R0805 pads are 1.025 by 1.4 mm with 1.825 mm center spacing, compatible with the 2.0 by 1.25 mm package. Ordering syntax is 100K + B (0.1%) + E (25 ppm/K) + EA (lead-free reel).
Use the same MPN and preferably one production lot for both positions. These remain two independent resistors, not a guaranteed ratio-tracking network: opposing 0.1% tolerances can shift the midpoint about 40 mV at an 80 V bus. Opposing 25 ppm/K coefficients can contribute about 1 mV/K. This selection does not change the existing midpoint accuracy claim or prove loaded regulation.
Primary source, pages 1–4 and package dimensions:
https://www.vishay.com/docs/28758/tnpw_e3.pdf

R3: Vishay CRCW080549R9FKEA, 49.9 ohm, 1%, 100 ppm/K, 0805. Standard-operation rating is 0.125 W and 150 V. Native footprint is the same R0805 geometry. Keep feedback before this resistor as drawn. Nominal 5 mA gives 1.25 mW, but resistor dissipation depends on total midpoint current, not only electrode current; 50 mA would already give approximately 0.125 W. Existing stability/thermal bench checks remain necessary.
Primary source, standard-operation ratings and order-code table:

C5: KEMET C0603C300J1GACTU. 30 pF, 5%, 100 VDC, C0G, 0603. Manufacturer code: 300 = 30 pF, J = 5%, 1 = 100 VDC, G = C0G, C = matte tin, TU = 7-inch reel. Body is 1.6 by 0.8 mm nominal; existing pads 0.90 by 0.95 mm at 1.55 mm center spacing accept this package. The 100 V rating preserves the existing requirement. No substitution to 50 V is implied.

BOOST

Native pads are 1.57 mm diameter with 1.02 mm holes on 2.54 mm pitch, matching the manufacturer's through-hole pattern. The -14 post is 8.13 mm long and overall pin length 13.46 mm per product page; preserve the exact lead-style suffix. J1 remains pin 1 ISO_12V_TO_BOTTOM, pin 2 HV_BOTTOM. J2 remains pin 1 HV_TOP, pin 2 HV_BOTTOM.
The manufacturer quotes 465 VAC and 4.7 A for its specified mated SSW/TSW configuration, not arbitrary loose jumper wires. This supplement selects the PCB header only. Use an insulated, labeled, strain-relieved bench harness; this unpolarized header does not prevent reversed connections or protect exposed energized contacts.
